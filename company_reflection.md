# Company Reflection

## Why do you think Focus Bear was created?

Jeremy was trying to stop a habit he did every day, checking his emails instead of doing his morning routine. He tried many different things to avoid it, but he could not stop it consistently. I think normal productivity apps did not work for him because they are not made for people with ADHD and autism, and they do not block distractions in a way that suits how his brain works. So he built Focus Bear with his own problem in mind, adding what he could not find in other apps. Later it turned out that many other neurodivergent people had the same problem.

## What problem is Focus Bear solving?

I think Focus Bear is solving the problem of people getting distracted while doing important work, and helping them focus on one task at a time. This is especially hard for people with ADHD and autism. When people get into hyperfocus, they often forget to take breaks, eat on time or stretch their body. Also, distracting apps like games and social media are always right in our hands, which makes them a very easy distraction. Focus Bear blocks these with smart app and website blocking. So basically, it helps people keep their focus and maintain a healthy routine at the same time.

## Why do you think this mission is important?

I feel this mission is important because it can help people with ADHD be more productive and do better at uni. People with ADHD are up to 40% less productive and 25% more likely to drop out of uni, so even a small improvement can make a big difference in someone's life. Most importantly, people with ADHD have much higher rates of suicide, so helping them handle daily struggles and feel less overwhelmed really matters. It is also important for the economy, because lost productivity from ADHD costs billions of dollars every year.

## How does Focus Bear's work align with your personal values or interests?

Focus Bear's work aligns closely with my values and interests. I have previously worked on a project in the mental health space, and I enjoy using my skills to solve real problems that real people face. After working at a big company, I realised I would learn a lot more in a startup with a small team, which is one of the reasons I chose this internship. I had also thought about building an app to help with social anxiety, and the AI features I had in mind are very similar to the ones in Focus Bear. The parts that interest me most are the task-related AI features and the AI motivational messages. On a personal level, I also try to focus on my own habits, like keeping a good sleep cycle and a healthy diet, so the idea of building healthy routines really connects with me.

## Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?

Yes, I relate to these challenges. When I try to focus on one task, I easily get distracted by other thoughts, doom scrolling, or switching between things. I lose track of time and then regret it later. In the few days since I started using the Focus Bear app, the focus sessions have helped me a lot to stay off my phone and focus on finishing my tasks.
