## How I Work Best

- I do my best work when I have a clear task, I know what the expected result is, and I get a long block of time to focus on it.
- I struggle when the requirements are vague or keep changing, or when I have to switch between many small tasks.

## Communication Style

- I prefer messages to be short and direct. If I need more detail, I will ask for it.
- I prefer async messages over calls for most things, because of my uni timetable.
- The best way to reach me is Discord.
- If you need something urgent, tag me on Discord or send me a direct message and mention that it is urgent. I may not reply right away during classes, but I will reply as soon as I am free.

## Collaboration & Teamwork

- I work best in a team when everyone knows who is doing what, and people are open about what they are stuck on.
- My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is when changes are made to shared work without telling anyone.
- If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means I am listening and thinking it through. I may come back with questions later in a message.

## Focus & Productivity

- I stay focused by breaking the work into small tasks, doing one at a time, and working in longer sessions without interruptions.
- Things that distract me are phone notifications and switching between too many things at once.
- The best way to support my productivity is to give me written instructions or a link to the issue, so I can refer back to it.

## Feedback & Growth

- I like feedback to be direct and specific, with an example if possible. Written feedback works well for me because I can go back to it.
- When I make a mistake, the best way to let me know is to tell me straight away and point to where it is. I would rather know early than find out later.
- I appreciate it when teammates explain the reason behind a decision, and when they are patient with questions while I am still learning the codebase.

## Fun Facts & Quirks

- One thing people often misunderstand about me is that short replies mean I am not interested. I just like to keep things brief.
- My work superpower is that I like to double check things. I read the instructions and the code carefully before I run or commit anything.
